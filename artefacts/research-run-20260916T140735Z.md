# Research answer: What is photosynthesis and what are its main stages?

Photosynthesis is a biological process by which autotrophic organisms convert light energy into the chemical energy needed to fuel their metabolism [1]. The process involves two main stages known as the light-dependent reactions and the light-independent reactions, or the Calvin cycle [7, 8]. During the light-dependent stage, sunlight and water are used to produce ATP, NADPH, and oxygen, which the light-independent stage then utilizes along with carbon dioxide to create sugars [7].

# Research answer: How do transformer-based language models handle long context windows?

Transformer-based language models handle and remember every token of their context using an attention operation that compares the latest token to all previous tokens [4]. However, this mechanism causes conventional models to become less efficient as the context grows [4]. To address these limitations and extend context windows, various architectural methodologies and strategies have been developed to enhance performance [1, 5, 6].

# Research answer: What were the main causes of the 2008 financial crisis?

The 2008 financial crisis originated from the U.S. subprime mortgage crisis and the subsequent collapse of the housing market [1, 8]. Major contributing factors included lax lending standards, U.S. government housing policies, and limited regulation of non-depository financial institutions [1]. Additional causes involved low mortgage and short-term interest rates, relaxed standards for mortgage loans, and an epicenter focused on mortgage-backed securities [2, 4].

# Research answer: What is the current state of fusion energy research?

Fusion power research involves developing devices that combine light atomic nuclei to generate electric power from released heat [1]. Current research features national and international collaboration projects, such as the ITER tokamak megaproject, which aims to demonstrate the feasibility of magnetic confinement fusion as a large-scale energy source [7]. Additionally, programs like ARPA-E ALPHA focus on advancing pulsed, intermediate-density fusion approaches to enable lower-cost pathways to economical fusion power [4].

# Research answer: How does CRISPR-Cas9 gene editing work at a molecular level?

CRISPR gene editing relies on a simplified version of a bacterial antiviral defense system to modify living genomes [1]. At the molecular level, the Cas9 nuclease is complexed with a synthetic guide RNA (gRNA) that matches and attaches to a specific target DNA sequence [1, 5]. Once bound, the Cas9 enzyme acts as molecular scissors to cut both strands of the DNA at that desired location, allowing existing genes to be removed or new ones added [1, 5].
